What Is
Wild Ledger?

Wild Ledger is a Web3 platform built on the XRP Ledger that empowers individuals to create, own, and operate independent communities around shared interests, bringing people together through their digital wallets rather than traditional accounts, centralized gatekeepers, or platform-controlled identities. Each community establishes its own culture, experiences, and membership, using LEAP—a common digital token across Wild Ledger—to welcome members, recognize contributions, and reward participation through a shared system of stars, badges, events, and challenges. Members earn and exchange value, build lasting records of their participation and achievements, and develop meaningful relationships within and across communities, while operators retain the authority to shape their communities and benefit from the value they create. At its heart, Wild Ledger transforms the traditional relationship between platforms, creators, and participants by placing ownership, recognition, economic opportunity, and verifiable history in the hands of the people who actually create the community's value.
