A beginner's guide

New to Crypto? Start Here.

You don't need to be a crypto expert to play the Game of LEAP. Here's what you need to get started.

Step 1

Get an XRP Ledger wallet.

You can use any wallet that supports the XRP Ledger and issued tokens like LEAP. Xaman and Bifrost Wallet are two options, but the choice is yours. We don't check what wallet app you use.

Creating a new XRPL account requires some XRP for the network's account reserve. Trust lines and transactions may also require XRP. Check the wallet's instructions before funding it.

Step 2

Find your XRPL address.

Your wallet has a public XRPL address that typically starts with r. That's the address you'll submit to join the game.

Only share your public address. Never share your recovery phrase, secret keys, or wallet password with anyone.

Step 3

Get ready to receive LEAP.

LEAP is a token issued on the XRP Ledger. To hold it, your wallet needs a LEAP trust line—a setting that tells the ledger your account can receive LEAP. Your wallet may call this “Add Token” or “Set Trust Line.”

When your first 100 LEAP are sent by an XRPL Check, you'll also need to accept (cash) the Check in a wallet or tool that supports XRPL Checks. An issued Check doesn't put LEAP into your balance until it's cashed.

Make sure the token's issuer is correct: rh7SidU91xGW2Za5RvoKiCgS2YB2psTBgq. Don't trust a token based on its name alone.

Step 4

Join the Game of LEAP.

Return to the home page, tap I Want to Play, and submit your XRPL address. Once your request is approved, you'll be sent your first 100 LEAP. Then you can start distributing LEAP and earning points.
