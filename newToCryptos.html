What you need to play

Follow these three steps to get ready to play the Game of LEAP.

Step 1

Get Xaman Wallet

Download Xaman Wallet from your app store. Xaman is the only wallet we've found that accurately displays LEAP.

Activating your new XRP Ledger account in Xaman requires some XRP for the network's account reserve. At current network fees, 3–4 XRP in your wallet could cover the reserve and a lifetime of normal LEAP play. That's a one-time wallet balance, not a monthly or yearly payment.

Step 2

Find your XRPL address.

Your account in Xaman has a public XRPL address that typically starts with r. That's the address you'll submit to join the game.

Only share your public address. Never share your recovery phrase, secret keys, or wallet password with anyone.

Step 3

Join the Game of LEAP.

Return to the home page and tap See the Player's Leaderboard. We'll walk you through the onboarding process on the leaderboard page.
