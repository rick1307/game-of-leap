Join the Wild Ledger team

Wild Ledger is growing, one community at a time. And we're looking for people who want to be part of it.

As a Community Operator, your job is to bring people together, give them reasons to participate, and put LEAP in their hands.

Here's the deal.

Wild Ledger gives you the tools. You bring the people, the ideas, and the energy.

Nobody's coming to build your community for you. Nobody's coming to keep it alive. That's your job.

Build something people want to be part of, and your community can grow. Ignore it, and it'll wither on the vine.

One community. One wallet. Forever. Every community has its own permanent Operating Wallet. You can hand over control someday, but that wallet never changes. That's how ownership works around here.

Connect the wallet you intend to operate with

There is no address-entry box. The XRPL wallet you connect here is the wallet Wild Ledger will provision as the community's first Community Operating Wallet.

This choice matters. Connect a wallet you control and expect to keep. Wild Ledger does not hold your private keys and cannot simply replace an established Operating Wallet if you lose access.

Know exactly what happens next

After a valid payment, you are done. Wild Ledger alerts the Owner. Community setup is usually completed within 24 hours. You do not need to send another form, email, or wallet address.

1 · RequestPayment validates from your Operating Wallet.
2 · AlertWild Ledger emails the Owner the wallet and payment hash.
3 · ProvisionOwner creates the community for that exact wallet.
4 · ReturnCheck your Member Record later. Your community is usually ready within 24 hours.
