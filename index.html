Strategy matters

Same LEAP. Different results.

Smart spread
10 recipients

10 wallets receive LEAP and have trust lines: 100 points. Next allotment unlocked.

Dead end
1 recipient

Give all 100 LEAP to one wallet. You earn at most 10 points, have 0 LEAP left, and your game ends unless you get more LEAP.

Each recipient contributes at most 3 receipt points and 7 trust-line points, once per player. Existing trust lines count. An issued but uncashed Check earns no receipt points.
