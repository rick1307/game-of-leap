Game of LEAP Protocol

Version 1.0

The Game of LEAP uses two XRPL Check memo values:

  • INITIAL — 100 LEAP. One successful claim per wallet. Uncashed Checks may be reissued.
  • REWARD — 200 LEAP. Repeatable upon earning 100 qualifying points.

All Checks originate from the designated LEAP Distributor.

That's the entire protocol.
